Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17th July 2026

We at Supergram Tech Pvt. Ltd. ("STPL," "we," "us," "our," or the "Company") take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, use, and may disclose your data when you use our mobile application, Himmin, and its various versions ("App" or "Platform"). References to "you," "your," or "user" mean any person or entity using the Platform.

This Privacy Policy is in effect alongside our Terms of Use ("Terms"), and by using the Platform, you agree to both. You also consent to our collection, use, and disclosure of your Personal Information as described here. Any capitalised terms not defined in this Privacy Policy carry the meaning given to them in our Terms. If you don't agree with this Privacy Policy, please refrain from using the Platform.

The information we collect and how we use it

The following table lists the information we collect from you and how we use it:

Information We Collect How We Use It

Log-in Data. When you create a Himmin account, we collect your User ID, mobile number, password, gender, and IP address. We may also estimate an age range for you, which helps us confirm you meet the minimum age requirement to use the Platform and to access certain features (together, "Log-in Data").

Content You Share. This includes everything you choose to make available to other users on Himmin, such as:

  • Personal information you voluntarily share, including photos, your profile picture, bio, username, quotes, and any opinions you express — political, religious, or otherwise.
  • Videos, comments, Drop Challenge entries, and any other posts you make on the Platform.

Information From Other Sources. We sometimes work with third parties — business partners, technical sub-contractors, analytics providers, and search/discovery partners — who may share information about you with us. We may combine this with the information we already have from your use of Himmin.

Log Data. When you use Himmin, certain information is collected automatically through cookies, log files, and similar tracking technologies, including:

  • Technical details like your mobile carrier, device configuration, browser or app version, IP address, and device ID.
  • What you've searched for or viewed on the app — search terms, profiles you've browsed, mini-apps or features used, and other content you've accessed.
  • Details about interactions with other users, such as who you've messaged and when, and how long the interaction lasted.
  • Metadata attached to your content, like the date, time, or location a photo or video was captured or uploaded.

Cookies. Himmin uses cookies to recognize you across sessions, which lets us give you a smoother experience and helps us improve the app over time. We collect data from these cookies directly from your device. Full details are available in our separate Cookie Policy.

Surveys. If you take part in any survey we run, we may ask for Personal Information — anything that could identify you. Where a third party runs the survey on our behalf, we'll let you know before you participate.
  • To set up and facilitate login to your Himmin account.
  • To notify you of changes to the Platform, including updates to this Privacy Policy.
  • To enable communication through the Platform, phone, and email — including user support and other services.
  • To enforce our Terms, policies, and rights, as well as those of our affiliates or other users.
  • To develop new features, improve existing ones, and act on user feedback and requests.
  • To personalize your experience based on language and location.
  • To run the Platform internally — troubleshooting, data analysis, testing, research, security, fraud detection, account management, and surveys.
  • To understand how you use Himmin and improve your overall experience.
  • To pseudonymize and aggregate your information (including Personal Information) for demographic analysis — e.g., by region, phone model, operating system, language, and app version — to better understand our user base.
  • To pseudonymize and aggregate your information for traffic statistics on how users engage with third-party services accessed through the Platform.
  • To create or upload linked profiles across other platforms operated by us or our Group.
  • To evaluate and improve the effectiveness of our advertising, marketing, and promotional activities.
User Search Data. We collect the searches you make within the Platform. To provide quick access to your previous searches. We use this to help you pick up where you left off.
Additional Account Security. When you register on Himmin, we collect your phone number and verify your identity through a One-Time Password (OTP) sent via SMS, which you enter to confirm your account. To verify your identity and secure your account. We request access to your SMS folder to automatically read the OTP during login, so you don't have to enter it manually.
Contacts List. We may request access to your device's contacts list. We always ask for your consent first, and you're free to deny this access. To suggest friends and invite your contacts to Himmin, and to notify you when someone from your contacts joins the Platform.
Customer Support Information. Any details you share with our support team when seeking help or assistance using the Platform. To provide support and assistance whenever you reach out to our team.

Device Data. This includes a range of technical information about the device you use to access Himmin, such as:

  • Device attributes — operating system and version, language, hardware/software details, device brand and model, screen resolution, battery level, signal strength, RAM, storage space, CPU details, browser type, and installed app/file types.
  • Device operations — how the app is being used on your device, such as whether it's running in the foreground or background.
  • Identifiers — unique device IDs and other identifiers tied to your apps or accounts.
  • Device signals — nearby Bluetooth, Wi-Fi access points, beacons, and cell towers, where applicable.
  • Data from device settings — information from permissions you've enabled, like GPS location, camera, or photo access.
  • Network details — your mobile operator or ISP, language, time zone, phone number, IP address, and connection speed.
  • Installed applications — other apps present on your device.
  • Media access — we may access your media gallery (photos, videos, audio) and available storage. We'll always ask for your consent first, and you can choose to deny this access.
  • To let you share media — audios, videos, and images — on and through the Platform.
  • To tailor Himmin to your device. So the app runs smoothly regardless of your phone's specs.
  • For camera configuration, ensuring recording features work properly on your device.
  • To check available storage before letting you download content for sharing via WhatsApp or Facebook.
  • To optimize your overall experience on the Platform.
  • To deliver the best possible video playback quality.
  • To let you share content through other apps installed on your device.
  • To verify your identity, helping us enforce our Terms and policies.
  • To improve the Platform over time.
  • To power your location-based feed.
  • To personalize content based on your language.
  • To improve camera lens quality and features.
Phone Call Logs. We may request access to your call logs to offer a missed-call verification option — an alternative to OTP if you're facing delays receiving your OTP during registration. This lets you confirm your phone number by simply giving a missed call instead. To verify your phone number through the missed-call mechanism, as a backup when OTP delivery is delayed.
Apple TrueDepth Camera Data. On supported devices, we may use data from Apple's TrueDepth camera to enhance the quality of camera lenses and filters. This data is processed in real time only, is never stored on our servers, and is never shared with third parties. To improve the accuracy and quality of camera lenses and filters using real-time TrueDepth data, without storing or sharing it.

Disclosure of Your Information

Here's how your information may be disclosed:

Content Visible to Others.

Anything you post publicly on your own profile or another user's profile — such as a comment — is visible to everyone, including search engines. Any information you choose to share on the Platform, including your profile details, is publicly accessible. Once you make content public, others may reshare it. Think carefully about what you share and with whom, since anyone who can see your activity can pass it along further — including to people outside the audience you originally intended, both on and off Himmin.

Other users can also create and share content that features you — like tagging you in a post or sharing a photo of you — with whatever audience they choose. We reserve the right to share any public content across social media or other online/offline platforms. We will never rent or sell your Personal Information to third parties, except on an anonymized basis, unless otherwise stated in our Terms.

Sharing With Our Group of Companies

We may share the information you provide, including Personal Information, with any member of our Group — meaning any entity that controls us, is controlled by us, or is under common control with us, whether directly or indirectly.

What You Share With Others

When you share content or communicate on Himmin, you choose who sees it. For instance, if you post Himmin content to Facebook, you decide the audience — a friend, a group, or everyone. Similarly, if you share content via WhatsApp or any other app on your device, you're choosing who receives it. We don't control, and aren't responsible for, how those recipients — the people you've chosen to share with through options like WhatsApp or Facebook — use the information you send them.

Sharing With Third Parties

We may share your information, including Personal Information, with select third parties, such as:

  • Business partners, suppliers, and sub-contractors ("Affiliates") — who may use this information to help deliver, understand, and improve our services, as well as their own.
  • Advertisers and ad networks, who need data to serve relevant ads. We don't share identifiable user information with advertisers — only aggregate data (for example, how many users in a certain age group engaged with an ad on a given day), which also helps them reach their intended audience more effectively.
  • Government bodies or law enforcement, where we believe in good faith it's necessary to comply with legal obligations or government requests, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the Company, our users, or the public — including addressing fraud, security, or technical issues.

We may also share your information with third parties in these situations:

  • Business transfers — if Himmin or substantially all of its assets are acquired, your data may be transferred as part of that deal. If a merger, acquisition, or sale means your information would become subject to a different privacy policy, we'll notify you in advance, giving you the chance to delete your account before the transfer if you choose.
  • Enforcing our Terms or other agreements with you.

Security Practices

We use appropriate technical and security measures to protect the information we collect. If you have a username and password to access Himmin, keeping those credentials confidential is your responsibility — please don't share your password with anyone.

Where We Store Your Information

We store your data across Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Microsoft Azure, on servers located in India and abroad. Each of these providers maintains its own security measures to protect against loss, misuse, or unauthorized alteration of data — details are available directly on their respective websites (aws.amazon.com, cloud.google.com, azure.microsoft.com), along with their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If we make any changes significant enough for you to know about, we'll post the revised version here.

Disclaimer

No method of transmitting data over the internet is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your Personal Information, we can't guarantee its security during transmission — any data you send us is at your own risk. Once we receive it, we apply strict security measures to help prevent unauthorized access.

Your Rights

You can delete content from your profile or delete your account entirely at any time. That said, we retain a record of your account activity even after deletion, in line with our data retention practices.

You can correct, update, or remove your Personal Information anytime by logging into your account. You can also opt out of promotional emails via the instructions in those messages — though you'll continue receiving essential system emails until your account is deleted. To delete your account and associated data, go to App Settings and select 'Request Account Deletion' / 'Delete My Data'. We may also delete accounts that have been inactive for an extended period.

Data Retention

We don't hold on to your Sensitive Personal Information (passwords and similar data classified as sensitive under applicable law) any longer than necessary. Videos and images you upload are generally stored for up to 180 days, after which they're automatically deleted — unless we need to retain them longer for legal compliance, contractual obligations with partners, or core business purposes. It's on you to save copies of anything you want to keep beyond that window.

You can delete your account anytime, and inactive accounts may be auto-deleted. Keep in mind that copies of public content may persist in cached pages, backups, or because other users saved or shared it. Once content is on the internet, copies can exist elsewhere indefinitely, even after you delete it from Himmin.

Third-Party Links

Himmin may link to websites operated by partners, advertisers, or others. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for them. Review their policies before sharing any personal data.

Music Licensing

As a short-video platform, we may use licensed music and audio content to enhance your experience creating and sharing videos. Where applicable, usage data related to music may be shared with our audio/music partners in an anonymised form.

Third-Party Embeds and Services

Some content on Himmin — like embedded videos or external media — is hosted by third parties, not by us. When you interact with this content, the third-party host receives data just as if you'd visited their site directly.

We may also work with third-party services that independently collect data to power certain in-app features (for example, camera lens or effects providers). Where applicable, we'll notify you of their terms before you use those features.

These third-party embeds and services aren't covered by this Privacy Policy — they're governed by the third party's own policy. By using them, you agree to their terms.

Sharing Personal Information With Third-Party Embeds

Some embeds on Himmin may ask you for Personal Information, like your email, through a form. We work to keep bad actors off the Platform, but once you submit information to a third party through such a form, we have no control over what they do with it — and as noted earlier, their actions aren't covered by this Privacy Policy.
